## Global specs (all four versions)

**Palette tokens**
- Navy `#0B1F4D`: base background
- Gold `#D4AF37`: titles, borders, VIP
- Emerald `#108981`: 1+1 badge, CTA button, cash
- Coral `#EF4444`: Early Bird and countdown only
- White `#FFFFFF`: body text

**Type:** Heebo Black / Rubik ExtraBold. 3D extruded gold titles (bevel, soft inner shadow). Pill badges with 8px gold stroke.

**Formats and safe zones**
- **9:16 (1080×1920):** master edit. Keep text inside the center 1080×1420, at least 250px clear at top and bottom for the Reels/TikTok UI.
- **1:1 (1080×1080):** reframe to center. Stack the badges vertically on the right third. Shorten on-screen text to one line where possible.
- **16:9 (1920×1080):** wide establishing shots. Text block on the left 40%, badges on the right. Can run 30s with a held endcard.

**VO:** warm, confident male Hebrew narrator. Plural address throughout ("קנו", "היכנסו"). About 2.5 words per second.

**Music:** music ducks −10 dB under the VO, with a 150ms attack.

**Captions:** burned-in Hebrew captions on every version. Most feed views are muted.

**Standard endcard (adapt per version)**
- Gold 3D logo "ההגרלה של החלום – שנה 9"
- Emerald pill "1+1 – כל כרטיס מוכפל בחינם"
- Package strip: "כרטיס ₪660 · מניין ₪4,200 · חי ₪6,480 · VIP ₪18,000"
- Coral tag "מוקדמים: $15,000 מזומן עד 11/11"
- Trust line "עם ישראל חי – הרכישה שלכם מחזקת את חיילי צה״ל ומשפחותיהם"
- URL **thedreamraffle.co.il**

## Build (Remotion)

All four scripts are built in `src/DreamRaffle/`, with 12 compositions: `DreamRaffle-V{1..4}-{9x16,1x1,16x9}`.

- **Images:** taken from thedreamraffle.co.il (apartment gallery, winners page, campaign mascot, Year 9 logo), stored in `public/raffle/`.
- **Music and SFX:** original, synthesized by `npm run raffle-audio` and stored in `public/raffle/audio/`.
- **Voiceover:** the lines live in `src/DreamRaffle/script.json` and are also burned in as captions. Run `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=sk_... npm run raffle-voiceover`, then re-render with `npm run raffle-render`. Once the clips exist, the music ducks under them automatically.
- **Arieh Lurie:** the site has no photo of the 2025 winner, so V1 shows a gold nameplate and V4 shows a gold "8" plaque.
