# Notes for video work in this repo

## The Dream Raffle brand
- Always use the official 9th-year logo: `my-video/public/dream/logo.png`
  ("The Dream RAFFLE · Back for the 9th Year", transparent PNG). Do not use
  the round "9th Consecutive Year" logo that is burned into older source
  footage; cover it where it appears.
- The logo's dark lettering disappears on dark backgrounds, so show it on a
  light badge (see `Logo` in `my-video/src/DreamRaffle/components.tsx`).
- The PNG was upscaled from a small supplied image; swap in a vector or
  high-res original if one becomes available.
