# The Dream Raffle — 30s 3D Commercial ("The Golden Key")

**Concept:** A glowing golden key drifts out of a raffle ticket and flies over Jerusalem at sunset. Along the way it lights up every place the raffle builds. It ends at the door of the dream apartment.
**Format:** 16:9 master (plus a 9:16 reframe), 24fps, 6 shots of 5s each.
**Style lock (append to every video prompt):**
`High-end 3D animated feature film style, Pixar / DreamWorks quality, warm golden-hour sunset, magical volumetric god rays, floating golden dust particles, rich warm palette of gold, honey, Jerusalem-stone beige and sky blue, shallow depth of field, cinematic 35mm lens, smooth motion, no text, no logos, no watermark.`

## Generative Video Prompts (English)

Each prompt below is written for **Kling / Runway Gen-3 / Sora**. Paste the prompt and add the style lock above at the end. Generate 5s clips at 16:9.

**Shot 1 — The Ticket Awakens**
> Extreme close-up of a paper raffle ticket resting on a rustic wooden table beside a sunlit window. The ticket begins to glow from within, and an ornate golden key slowly rises out of it, spinning gently, surrounded by swirling golden sparkles. Slow push-in camera, rack focus from the ticket to the floating key, warm sunset light streaming through the window.

**Shot 2 — Flight Over Jerusalem**
> A glowing golden key flies out of an open window and soars over the Jerusalem skyline at golden hour, with warm Jerusalem-stone buildings, the ancient Old City walls and a golden dome glowing in the distance. Sweeping drone-style follow shot that rises into a crane move, a golden light trail behind the key, birds in silhouette, volumetric sun rays through soft clouds.

**Shot 3 — Lighting the Way**
> A glowing golden key glides past a yeshiva window where young men study Torah around a wooden table, and warm golden light spills over them as they look up smiling. Quick match-cut: the key glides past a cheerful young Israeli soldier opening a care package on a sunny street, and the scene lights up warmly. Fast lateral tracking camera, sparkles blooming wherever the key passes, uplifting and heartfelt.

**Shot 4 — The Star Over the City**
> Golden light trails rise from across Jerusalem at sunset and weave together in the sky into a giant radiant Star of David made of glowing light, with a waving Israeli flag in the foreground. Slow 180-degree orbit that pulls back to reveal the whole city bathed in golden light, epic and awe-inspiring, lens flare, god rays.

**Shot 5 — The Dream Door**
> A glowing golden key swoops down to the elegant wooden door of a luxury penthouse, inserts itself into the lock and turns. The door swings open to reveal a breathtaking, fully furnished modern apartment with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Jerusalem at sunset: warm wood, marble, plush sofas and soft gold accents. First-person POV follow that becomes a smooth dolly-in through the doorway, with magical dust particles in the light.

**Shot 6 — End Card Background (optional)**
> A luxury apartment balcony overlooking Jerusalem at sunset, golden sky, gentle breeze moving sheer curtains, calm and aspirational, static locked-off camera with very subtle drift, empty negative space in the center for a title overlay.

**Negative prompt** (for Runway or Kling, where supported):
`text, letters, watermark, logo, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, flicker, low quality, blurry, photorealistic live action`
